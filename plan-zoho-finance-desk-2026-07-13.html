Risks

Where this can fail

Click each risk for the mitigation.

Show scope, freshness, filters, formulas, currencies, source counts, and links on every analytical result. Build a real-data finance evaluation suite before adding autonomy.
Default to draft mode. Keep all credentials and policy backend-side. Use risk- and amount-based approvals, idempotency keys, duplicate detection, and post-write verification.
Do not launch invoices, expenses, reconciliation, close, and tax simultaneously. Win collections first, with 3–5 design-partner users and weekly workflow reviews.
Move core workflows into deterministic playbooks and structured UI states. Let the model investigate and explain; let typed services execute controlled steps.
Expose organization and schema context, tolerate custom fields, maintain mapping diagnostics, and route low-confidence cases into an exception queue instead of guessing.
Every queue item needs an owner, status, due date, evidence, activity history, and resolution. Otherwise Divo becomes another reporting surface people check and abandon.
